About Me

I’m a Lead Machine Learning Scientist at Mind Foundry, with 10+ years’ experience spanning applied research and delivery leadership in machine learning, autonomous systems, robotics in defence and national security. I lead cross-functional teams of ML scientists and engineers, line manage senior technical staff, and work closely with customers and commercial stakeholders to turn ambiguous operational problems into deployed ML solutions — spanning computer vision, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, electronic surveillance, and localisation and tracking.

Before moving into industry, I spent seven years as a researcher at the University of Bristol working on multi-agent systems, decision-making, and autonomous vehicles, including the UK’s flagship driverless car project (Venturer) and a PhD in Aerospace Engineering focused on optimal routing for commercial formation flight.

Education

2011 - 2015
PhD, Aerospace Engineering
University of Bristol

Thesis: “Optimal Routing and Assignment for Commercial Formation Flight”.

Developed a scalable analytic-geometric approach to commercial formation flight routing, enabling fast cost estimation and large-scale fleet assignment via Mixed Integer Linear Programming. Demonstrated fuel savings of 8-13% in a Transatlantic case study, with consistent gains shown across flights of varied distance, geography, and formation size.
